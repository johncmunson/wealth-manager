- [Atom Finance - Accessible investing with linked accounts](https://plaid.com/customer-stories/atom-finance/): Investment-account linking example relevant if the brokerage imports outside holdings for portfolio context.

- [OneGold - Propelling growth of precious metal investing](https://plaid.com/customer-stories/onegold/): Investing product example focused on linking bank accounts for ACH-funded investments.
- [Public - Streamlined onboarding & secure authentication](https://plaid.com/customer-stories/public/): Brokerage-adjacent example for authenticating and funding investment accounts quickly and securely.
- [Robinhood: Enabling instant trades with ACH fraud prevention](https://plaid.com/customer-stories/robinhood/): Direct brokerage example for ACH funding, instant availability, and fraud/return-risk reduction.
- [Stash - Move money quickly with easy onboarding](https://plaid.com/customer-stories/stash/): Investing app example for onboarding, money movement, and fraud reduction with Plaid.
- [Ziggma - Simple portfolio management with connected accounts](https://plaid.com/customer-stories/ziggma/): Portfolio-management example using connected investment accounts for monitoring and alerts.
