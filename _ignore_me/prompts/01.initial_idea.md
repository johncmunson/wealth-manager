I would like to build an online agentic brokerage that is built on top of Alpaca and places emphasis on long-term investing. Users should be able sign-up, fund their account, create a portfolio strategy, place orders and trades, rebalance their portfolio, conduct basic market research, etc.

The core user experience will be the primarily be the user interacting with a chat-based wealth manager AI agent, but there will still likely be other supplemental and more traditional UI and pages for certain things.

The wealth manager agent will have skills and tools at its disposal enabling it to carry out all core investing and trading tasks, such as market research, executing trades, balancing a portfolio in accordance with the portfolio strategy, etc.

Furthermore, the user should be able to coordinate with the wealth manager agent in order to schedule recurring, out-of-band operations, such as automated portfolio rebalancing according to a set of predefined rules. For example, "once a quarter" or "when the portfolio drifts over 10% away from the target". Automated tax-loss harvesting and dollar-cost averaging are also good candidates for scheduled background operations.

Primary goal: An online agentic brokerage that emphasizes long-term investing and doesn't overwhelm the user with a million different features and strategies. It focuses on the basics and does them really fucking well.

Non-goals: An online brokerage that does anything and everything. Utilizing or taking advantage of the entirety of capabilities exposed by Alpaca.
